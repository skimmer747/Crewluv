# Crewluve Trip Map: The Latest Event, Shown Large

Status: approved by Todd on 15 Nov 2026, from sketches of three places for it. It builds on the best guesses design (`2026-11-15-crewluve-facts-best-guesses-design.md`), whose events it shows.

## 1. What it shows

- **The newest event of the flight in the air,** large: "Crossed into Canada", "Out over the Pacific", "Halfway" or "Closest to home, 35 mi".
- **Under it, how long ago:** "Just now" in its first minute, then "12 min ago", to the nearest minute.
  - A best guess says so: "About now", "About 40 min ago".
  - At a moment of the replay, where "ago" means nothing, its clock time: "10:37 AM", "About 10:37 AM".
- **When:** from the flight's first event until it reaches the gate. It stays until the next event. Before the first event, on the ground and on a drive, there's none.

## 2. Where

- **The card,** in the top corner of its map, which is empty in flight. At the largest text sizes the card's chips move below its map, and the banner goes first among them.
- **The full-screen map,** centred under the pill. The camera keeps the plane clear of it.
- **The card's small bottom chip and the facts sheet's first line stop repeating events.** They read what's below, a sunrise or sunset coming up, or the landing countdown. The headline the website shares (`facts-cases.json`) is unchanged.

## 3. VoiceOver

The card's one sentence ends with the banner: "Latest: crossed into Canada, 12 minutes ago." On the full-screen map the banner reads that sentence.

## 4. Not in this design

- A flag on the map where the event happened.
- The website.
